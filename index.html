Research

🤖

Machine Learning

Our research focuses on developing novel machine learning algorithms including deep neural networks, reinforcement learning, and transfer learning. We work on both theoretical foundations and practical applications across various domains.

🧠

Artificial Intelligence

We explore advanced AI methodologies including natural language processing, computer vision, robotics, and intelligent systems. Our goal is to create AI systems that can understand and interact with the world more effectively.

📊

Data Science

We develop innovative approaches for analyzing large-scale, complex datasets. Our research includes statistical modeling, data mining, visualization techniques, and distributed computing systems.

🔬

Computational Biology

Applying computational and mathematical methods to biological problems. We work on genomics, proteomics, drug discovery, and personalized medicine using advanced computational techniques.

🏥

Healthcare AI

Developing AI solutions for healthcare applications including medical image analysis, diagnostic systems, treatment recommendation, and electronic health record analysis.

🚗

Autonomous Systems

Research in autonomous vehicles, drones, and robotic systems. We focus on perception, planning, control, and safety aspects of autonomous systems in complex environments.
